Common Questions

What businesses usually ask before starting.

How long does a typical website or web app take to build?

A marketing website usually takes two to four weeks. A full web application or SaaS platform typically takes six to twelve weeks, depending on scope.

Do you build on WordPress, or custom code?

It depends on your needs — for content-driven sites, an existing platform can be the right call; for applications and portals, we build custom for better performance and flexibility.

Can you redesign or modernize our existing site?

Yes — we regularly take over and modernize existing websites and applications, migrating them onto a more current, maintainable foundation.

Do you provide hosting and ongoing maintenance?

Yes, as an ongoing engagement once your project launches — security updates, performance monitoring, and continued support.

What if our project needs to scale later?

That's exactly what the architecture is built for from day one — we design with growth in mind, not just the initial launch.

Do you build e-commerce stores?

Yes — from straightforward online stores to custom-built platforms with specific inventory, booking, or membership requirements.
