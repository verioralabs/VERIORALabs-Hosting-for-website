Common Questions

What businesses usually ask before starting.

Is this "real" AI, or just a chatbot script?

It depends on the use case — we build everything from simple automated responses to more advanced AI-driven document processing and reporting, matched to what your business actually needs.

Will AI replace our staff?

No — the goal is to remove repetitive, low-value work so your team can spend time on things that actually need human judgment.

How do you handle data privacy with AI tools?

We assess data sensitivity case by case and choose tools and configurations that keep your business data appropriately protected.

What if we don't have clean data yet?

That's common — we often start with a data clean-up phase before automation, since the quality of the input directly affects the result.

How long does an AI automation project take?

Simple automations can launch in a few weeks; more involved integrations with existing systems typically take six to ten weeks.

Can AI integrate with our existing software?

In most cases, yes — we design integrations around the systems you already use rather than asking you to replace them.
