Common Questions

What businesses usually ask before starting.

Do you only advise, or can you also build?

Both — many consulting engagements lead directly into a build with our team, but there's no obligation to continue past the advisory stage.

We already have a dev team — can you still help?

Yes — we often work alongside existing internal or agency teams as an outside strategic perspective, not a replacement.

How long does a consulting engagement take?

It depends on scope — a focused strategy session can be a single engagement, while a full roadmap typically takes two to four weeks.

Do you recommend specific vendors or tools?

Only when it's genuinely the right fit — our recommendations aren't tied to any reseller or partnership arrangement.

Is this a one-time engagement or ongoing?

Either — some clients need a single strategic review, others prefer ongoing quarterly technology advisory.
