Common Questions

What partners usually ask us first.

Who owns the code and IP once a project is delivered?

You do, in full. Every engagement is covered by an NDA and IP assignment from day one — nothing we build on your behalf is retained or reused by us.

How is confidentiality handled with our clients?

Under white-label engagements, we operate entirely behind your brand — no Veriora Labs branding, watermarks, or direct contact with your client unless you want it that way.

Can you share examples of past dedicated-team or offshore work?

Much of our team's prior work — including engagements our leadership has led — is protected under client NDAs and can't be shared publicly. We're glad to speak candidly about relevant experience and approach on a call, and can walk through anonymized examples of the type of systems we've built.

What does the communication process look like?

You get a single point of contact and a reporting cadence we agree on together — weekly syncs, async updates, or embedded in your own project management tool, whatever fits how your team runs.

Can we start small and scale up later?

Yes — most partnerships start with one engineer or a single project, then scale into a larger dedicated team once the fit is proven. There's no minimum commitment to get started.

What does onboarding a new partnership look like?

After an initial call, we sign an NDA, agree on scope and engagement model, and get access set up — most partnerships are ready to start delivering within one to two weeks.
